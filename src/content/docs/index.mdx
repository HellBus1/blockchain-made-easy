## Why Blockchain Made Easy?

Traditional finance and crypto whitepapers are filled with dense jargon, conflicting buzzwords, and abstract math. **Blockchain Made Easy** breaks every concept down into tangible mechanisms.

## 5-Track Curriculum Overview

1. **Foundations**: What money is, double-spending, distributed ledgers, hashing, mining, and block sealing.
2. **Wallets & Security**: Public addresses, private keys, 12-word seed phrases, and avoiding scams.
3. **Exchanges & Buying**: Crossing the fiat bridge, matching engines, order books, and proof-of-reserves.
4. **Ethereum & Smart Contracts**: Programmable money, gas, tokens, stablecoins, and decentralized finance.
5. **Market Literacy & Trading**: Reading candlesticks, order types, risk management, and paper trading.
