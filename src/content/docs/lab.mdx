Welcome to the **Simulation Lab**. Every core mechanism taught in Blockchain Made Easy is backed by an interactive laboratory component you can tweak, stress-test, and observe in real-time.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="1. SHA-256 Hash Playground" icon="seti:code">
    Type any text and watch the 256-bit cryptographic fingerprint change instantaneously. Experience the *avalanche effect* where changing one single comma completely randomizes the output.

    [Explore in Chapter 1.5 &rarr;](/learn/foundations/05-cryptographic-hashing/)
  </Card>

  <br />

</Card>

<Card title="3. Proof-of-Work Mining Engine" icon="seti:cpu">

  <br />
