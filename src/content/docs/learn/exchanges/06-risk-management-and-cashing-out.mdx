### The Golden 1% Risk Rule & Position Sizing

The cardinal sin of amateur traders is risking 20%, 50%, or 100% of their total bankroll on a single "hot coin tip". 

In financial probability, a streak of 4 or 5 bad trades is statistically inevitable over any multi-month timeline. If you risk 20% per trade, five consecutive losses destroys 67% of your capital—requiring a massive **+200% return just to break even!**

```
THE ASYMMETRY OF LOSS (THE BREAK-EVEN TRAP):

 Portfolio Loss:   -10% ──► Need +11% to break even
 Portfolio Loss:   -25% ──► Need +33% to break even
 Portfolio Loss:   -50% ──► Need +100% to break even
 Portfolio Loss:   -80% ──► Need +400% to break even!
```

Professional risk managers follow the **1% Rule**: **Never risk losing more than 1% of your total liquid net worth on any single trade.**

#### The Position Sizing Formula
To calculate exactly how many tokens to buy, use this mathematical formula:

```text
Position Size = (Total Portfolio × 0.01) / (Entry Price - Stop-Loss Price)
```

- Total Portfolio: **$10,000** (Max risk at 1% = **$100**)
- Bitcoin Entry Price: **$68,000**
- Planned <Term id="stop-loss">Stop-Loss</Term>: **$66,000** (Risk per BTC = $2,000)
- Position Size: **$100 / $2,000 = 0.05 BTC** ($3,400 trade size)

If Bitcoin drops to $66,000 and hits your stop-loss, you exit automatically. You lose exactly **$100 (1%)** of your capital, preserving 99% of your ammunition to trade another day!

### The Mathematical Secret: Risk-to-Reward (R:R)

You do not need an 80% or 90% win rate to generate outstanding fiat income in financial markets. In fact, professional trend followers often win only **40% of their trades** and still compound substantial fortunes:

```
THE POWER OF A 1:3 RISK-TO-REWARD RATIO (OVER 10 TRADES):

 · Wins: 4 trades × $300 profit = +$1,200
 · Losses: 6 trades × $100 loss   = -$600
 ──────────────────────────────────────────
 Net Realized Profit = +$600 (Healthy +6% growth!)
```

By ensuring that every trade setup offers at least **$2.50 to $3.00 of upside for every $1.00 of risk**, you can be wrong more than half the time and still consistently grow your fiat wealth!

### Tax Hygiene & Legal Status: Keeping Your Profits Clean

As you generate substantial income from cryptocurrency, maintaining clean accounting records is mandatory to prevent banking freezes or tax penalties.

#### Indonesian Regulatory Framework (Bappebti & DJP)
- Regulated domestic crypto exchanges automatically withhold **0.10% PPh Final (Income Tax)** and **0.11% PPN (Value Added Tax)** per transaction.
- Because it is a *Final Tax*, you simply report your year-end exchange balance and capital holdings on your annual **SPT Tahunan (Tax Return)** under assets (*Harta*), accompanied by your official exchange tax statement.
- Using regulated fiat gateways ensures that when large deposits land in your bank account, you have complete legal documentation proving the funds originated from legitimate virtual asset trading.

#### Global / US Tax Rules (IRS Capital Gains)
- In the United States and Europe, every crypto-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat swap is treated as a taxable capital disposal.
- Keep comprehensive CSV transaction histories or use tax software (CoinTracker, Koinly) to calculate Short-Term vs Long-Term capital gains deductions.
