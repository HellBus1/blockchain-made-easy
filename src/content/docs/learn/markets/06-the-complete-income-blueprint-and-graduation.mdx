### The Weekly & Monthly Income Routine

Professional investors do not spend 14 hours a day glued to volatile Twitter feeds. They operate on a calm, structured schedule:

#### Weekly Routine (30 Minutes on Sunday)
1. **Review Macro Market Structure**: Check the 1-Day chart of Bitcoin and Ethereum. Is the trend intact?
2. **Review Grid Trading Bots**: Are active trading corridors still bounded by the upper and lower price range? Harvest accumulated grid arbitrage profits.
3. **Check Gas Schedules**: If performing Layer 2 yield compounding or contract interactions, execute them during Sunday off-peak hours when base fees are lowest.

#### Monthly Routine (1 Hour at Month-End)
1. **Harvest Accrued Yields**: Claim passive yields from liquid staking pools, Aave lending vaults, and AMM pools on Base/Arbitrum.
2. **Execute the Fiat Off-Ramp**: Convert a fixed percentage (e.g. 30%–50%) of all realized monthly profits into fiat via your regulated domestic exchange or P2P merchant rail directly to your bank account.
3. **Lock Away Foundation Reserves**: Transfer 30% of remaining profits into cold storage Bitcoin and Ethereum.
