### Key Concept: The Glass Mailbox

- **Public Key (Address)**: Like a transparent mailbox on the street. Anyone can view what is inside and drop a payment into the slot.
- **Private Key**: The only key that unlocks the door to withdraw funds. Never share this with anyone.
