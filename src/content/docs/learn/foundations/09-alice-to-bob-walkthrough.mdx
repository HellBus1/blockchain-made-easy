## Summary of the 6 Acts

1. **Act 1 • The Problem**: Traditional money is controlled by a single bank that owns the private database. They control settlement speed, fees, and access.
2. **Act 2 • The Breakthrough**: The <Term id="blockchain">Blockchain</Term> replaces the single bank with thousands of independent <Term id="node">nodes</Term> running an identical synchronized notebook.
3. **Act 3 • Minting Supply**: Brand-new coins enter circulation strictly as protocol rewards given to miners and validators who seal blocks via <Term id="coinbase-tx">Coinbase transactions</Term>.
4. **Act 4 • Digital Identity**: Users claim accounts using cryptographic keypairs: <Term id="public-key">Public addresses</Term> (glass mailboxes) and <Term id="private-key">Private keys</Term> (unforgeable digital signatures).
5. **Act 5 • The Fiat Bridge**: Regulated exchanges (<Term id="fiat-bridge">fiat gateways</Term>) allow buyers with Rupiah (IDR) or USD to match with sellers, exchanging fiat for digital tokens off-chain.
6. **Act 6 • On-Chain Settlement**: By withdrawing coins from the exchange to your personal wallet (<Term id="self-custody">self-custody</Term>), your ownership is sealed onto the global blockchain forever.
