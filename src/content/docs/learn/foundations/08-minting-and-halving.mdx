In the traditional banking world, central banks (like the Federal Reserve or Bank Indonesia) can expand the monetary base by printing currency or expanding digital reserves at their discretion.

In a decentralized cryptocurrency like Bitcoin, **there is no central bank governor**. All issuance is dictated strictly by open-source computer code written into the protocol.

---

## Where Do Coins Come From? The Coinbase Transaction

When a miner or validator seals a block, the software protocol allows them to insert a special transaction at the very top of the block called the <Term id="coinbase-tx">Coinbase Transaction</Term>.

```
Block #102 Transaction List
┌─────────────────────┬───────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬─────────────┐
│ Tx Type             │ From                  │ To Address           │ Amount      │
├─────────────────────┼───────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼─────────────┤
│ ✨ MINT REWARD      │ PROTOCOL (New Supply) │ bc1q_miner_alpha     │ +3.125 BTC  │
│ Regular Transfer    │ bc1q_alice88          │ bc1q_bob99           │ 0.400 BTC   │
│ Regular Transfer    │ bc1q_charlie          │ bc1q_dave77          │ 1.150 BTC   │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴─────────────┘
```

The protocol creates these coins out of thin air according to an unalterable schedule. This is how all native Bitcoin originally entered circulation!

## The 21 Million Hard Cap

Because the reward cuts in half every 4 years (50 &rarr; 25 &rarr; 12.5 &rarr; 6.25 &rarr; 3.125 ...), the total supply forms a mathematical converging series:

```text
210,000 × (50 + 25 + 12.5 + 6.25 + 3.125 + ...) = 21,000,000 BTC
```

Around the year **2140**, the 33rd halving will reduce the block reward to zero. At that point, all 21,000,000 Bitcoins will have been minted, and miners will sustain operations purely through voluntary transaction fees paid by network users.

<Aside type="tip" title="Absolute Digital Scarcity">
  Before Bitcoin, digital items could always be duplicated. By enforcing a mathematical cap through
  decentralization, Bitcoin introduced the world's first **verifiably scarce digital asset**.
</Aside>
